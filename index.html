About

I am a PhD student in Computer Engineering at the Budapest University of Technology and Economics (BME), working on modern computer vision systems for autonomous vehicles. My work sits where deep learning meets geometry: visual SLAM and odometry, semantic segmentation and representation pretraining, and — most recently — traversability mapping of unstructured environments for path planning, which I spent a year on as a researcher at Shizuoka University.

Before the PhD I completed my MSc and BSc in Electrical Engineering at BME, with theses on deep learning-based SLAM and on GPU-accelerated optical flow.
